
# Rivo Phone

Prebuilt Rivo dialer (`user-grinch/RivoPhoneApp`) installed as a privileged
system app. Replaces the Google dialer and is set as the default dialer.

## Project Status: Temporarily Paused

The Rivo Phone integration is currently **on hold until further notice**.

We genuinely appreciate the work and effort put into the Rivo Phone project by its developers. We respect their work and their decision to take the project in a different direction. It's their project, and they have every right to make their own decisions.

However, we also believe that the open-source community deserves transparency and respect. Rivo was introduced with the promise of remaining **open-source and ad-free**, and many people supported the project with those expectations.

Changing those commitments is disappointing, especially for the community members who trusted, supported, and believed in the project's original vision.

**Open source is more than just publishing code. It's about transparency, trust, collaboration, and respecting the community that stands behind a project.**

We stand with the open-source community and believe that promises made to users and contributors should matter. While we respect the developers' choices, we also have our own commitments and values to uphold.

## What's Next?

We have decided to move forward with an alternative dialer that better aligns with our vision and the expectations of our community.

We are currently working on the replacement, and **the repository name will also be changed soon** to reflect this transition.

More details will be shared once the replacement is finalized and ready for integration.

Thank you to everyone who continues to support us and believes in what we are building.

**Our commitment to the community and open-source values remains unchanged.**

## Still Want to Use Rivo Phone?

For anyone who still wants to use Rivo Phone, the existing version referenced in this integration remains available as a free and open-source option, as far as its current availability allows. We are still using this earlier version, and it continues to meet our needs.

You are welcome to use the existing integration while it remains available. Please note that we cannot guarantee its future availability or maintenance.
